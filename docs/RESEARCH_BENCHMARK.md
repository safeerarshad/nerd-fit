# Research benchmark

Research date: 2026-09-23 (Asia/Calcutta). Scope: Milestone 0, public product behavior and repository inspection. This is a clean-room research record, not an assertion of feature parity or a reconstruction of competitor algorithms. No competitor code, assets, copy, or layouts were imported.

## Evidence rules

- **[OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR]** means a claim made by MacroFactor's own public documentation. This verifies what the vendor documents, not independent clinical efficacy or installed-app behavior.
- **[MACROPHASE DOCUMENTATION]** means a claim in MacroPhase's public documentation or README. Application source did not independently confirm these claims in the inspected repositories.
- **[UNVERIFIED / PROPRIETARY]** identifies details not established by primary evidence, conflicting documentation, or unpublished internals.
- **[NERD FIT DESIGN DECISION]** identifies an original requirement or proposed implementation direction. Numerical choices require the separate algorithm evidence/specification and tests.

MacroFactor Nutrition is the benchmark here. Search results for the separately documented MacroFactor Workouts and Watch apps were not treated as Nutrition feature evidence. Pages are live and may change; URLs below identify the sources inspected. GitHub trees are pinned where possible.

## MacroFactor: coaching and planning

| Required area | Documented behavior and evidence | Limit / Nerd Fit implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weight trend | [OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] Missing scale readings between observations are linearly interpolated; a moving average gives more weight to recent readings. Trend changes feed expenditure and targets, reducing reactions to short-lived water/gut-content changes. The vendor recommends daily or at least three weekly weigh-ins. [Weight Trend](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/dashboard/weight_trend) | [UNVERIFIED / PROPRIETARY] This page does not disclose complete smoothing coefficients. Do not label MacroFactor's filter EMA-9, EMA-20, or Kalman based on another vendor's comparison. |
| Expenditure estimation | [OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] The estimate uses reported food energy and weight-trend change, rather than whether intake matched assigned targets. [Interpreting expenditure](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/26-how-should-i-interpret-changes-to-my-energy-expenditure). The displayed change rate summarizes 20 days as a weekly value. [Change Rate](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/19-change-rate) | [UNVERIFIED / PROPRIETARY] A public conceptual energy-balance explanation is not the production estimator, complete tissue-energy model, or uncertainty model. |
| Expenditure V3 | [OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] V3 is recommended over V1/V2; documented improvements include less overcorrection, earlier response to real changes, better missing-data tolerance, and approximately 10% prospective accuracy improvement. The chart's flux range is explicitly not a statistical confidence interval. [Expenditure Version](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/74-expenditure-version) | [UNVERIFIED / PROPRIETARY] Accuracy percentages are vendor-reported comparisons, not independent validation or a benchmark Nerd Fit has met. |
| Missing data | [OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] Current V3 minimums are four nutrition days per seven and one weight day per week; more consistent logging is recommended. Insufficient data pauses updates and holds the prior estimate. Zero-energy unlogged days are ignored unless explicitly marked fasting. [Expenditure Version](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/74-expenditure-version) | [NERD FIT DESIGN DECISION] Preserve distinct missing, partial, complete, estimated-complete, and fasting states. Never treat an absent record as zero. Do not copy these thresholds without testing the original Nerd Fit estimator. |
| V3 missing-intake inference | [OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] The V3 article describes internal inference of intake on missing days; its reported accuracy and simulations concern that vendor's own data. [V3 explanation](https://macrofactor.com/expenditure-v3/) | [UNVERIFIED / PROPRIETARY] Inference equations and fitted parameters are not exposed sufficiently to reproduce the system. Generic help wording that an unlogged day is skipped must not be stretched into a claim that V3 performs no internal inference. |
| Partial logging | [OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] Logging some but not all intake biases expenditure downward. The help page describes a 21-day nutrition window and a check-in module that can ask users to mark suspected partial days so the algorithm ignores them. It recommends estimating omitted intake or otherwise excluding the day. [Partial logging](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/241-what-is-partial-logging) | [NERD FIT DESIGN DECISION] Retain partial food records for the diary while excluding them from complete-intake inference. Ask for confirmation before labeling a low-intake day partial; low calories alone do not establish missing food. |
| Adherence-neutral coaching | [OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] Targets can update even when the user eats above or below their plan. The product avoids shaming visual elements and automatic compensatory restriction. Actual intake, rather than the old prescription, is relevant to expenditure. [Adherence neutrality](https://macrofactor.com/adherence-neutral/) | [NERD FIT DESIGN DECISION] Explain measured behavior neutrally. Do not gate coaching on a target-adherence score or impose repayment of excess calories. |
| Weekly check-ins | [OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] Coached/collaborative programs offer weekly target recommendations on a configurable day; recommendations can be declined. Contextual coaching modules are skippable and can address fasting, partial logs, and breaks. A fast check-in option bypasses modules. [Check-ins](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/247-introduction-to-check-ins-and-coaching-modules) | [NERD FIT DESIGN DECISION] Store the inputs, explanation, proposal, and accept/keep decision. Only acceptance changes prescribed targets; recommendations must be reproducible. |
| Dynamic maintenance | [OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] When trend weight is within about 0.7 kg of the selected maintenance weight, recommendations track expenditure. Outside that band, the documented adjustment aims at 0.15% bodyweight/week toward the target. [Dynamic maintenance](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/125-how-does-dynamic-maintenance-work-in-macrofactor) | [NERD FIT DESIGN DECISION] Use an independently justified maintenance deadband, gentle corrections, and hysteresis; competitor constants are observations, not safety evidence. |
| Goal rate selection | [OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] Users choose lose/maintain/gain, target weight, and an adjustable rate with a recommended visual region. [New goal](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/90-set-a-new-goal). The app does not automatically accelerate the desired rate to recover schedule slippage. [Strict timelines](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/202-what-should-i-do-if-im-pursuing-a-goal-with-a-strict-timeline) | [NERD FIT DESIGN DECISION] Persist percentage bodyweight/week and derive kg/lb estimates from current appropriate trend weight. Show an uncertain goal window, not a promise. |
| Weekly calorie budgets | [OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] Coached/collaborative weekly budgets depend on expenditure and goal; changing one day's allocation changes other days to maintain the weekly sum. [Weekly Budget](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/macro_program/weekly_budget) | [NERD FIT DESIGN DECISION] Store the weekly budget and explicit allocation; rounding must preserve the exact weekly total. |
| Calorie shifting | [OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] Coached plans support even distribution or modest shifts to selected days. [Coached options](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/34-what-are-the-different-program-options-in-coached-mode). Collaborative users can manually lock days and redistribute calories, including overages/underages; this rollover is deliberate, not automatic/default. [Adjusting allocations](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/108-how-to-adjust-calorie-targets-to-account-for-overages-or-to-roll-over-unused-calories) | [NERD FIT DESIGN DECISION] Offer planned higher/lower days with visible weekly totals and floors. Do not automatically transform an overage into future food restriction. |
| Coached/collaborative/manual | [OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] Coached adjusts targets using preferences and progress. Collaborative updates the weekly energy budget while allowing daily macro/energy control. Manual leaves targets to the user while retaining analytics. [Program Styles](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/macro_program/program_styles) | [NERD FIT DESIGN DECISION] Start with one understandable automatic plan plus advanced controls; additional modes must share goal and history models. |
| Protein and macro generation | [OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] Preferences and exercise type influence coached macros; users select a protein level and diet style. [Coached options](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/34-what-are-the-different-program-options-in-coached-mode). A November 2025 update publishes revised lifter protein coefficients in grams/kg fat-free mass, including moderate/high maintenance-gain values of 2.35/2.75 and cutting values of 2.5/3.0. [Expenditure modifiers and protein update](https://macrofactor.com/expenditure-modifiers/) | [UNVERIFIED / PROPRIETARY] These published coefficients do not establish the entire macro generator. [NERD FIT DESIGN DECISION] Use a separate evidence-backed protein policy, explicit fat constraints, and energy-consistent carbohydrate remainder. |
| Calorie safeguards | [OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] Coached options document a standard 1,200 kcal floor, an individualized lower setting, and an opt-out. Collaborative mode omits the coached daily calorie and fat safeguards. [Coached options](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/34-what-are-the-different-program-options-in-coached-mode), [Program Styles](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/macro_program/program_styles) | [NERD FIT DESIGN DECISION] Do not present a competitor's floor or opt-out as a universal safe intake. Nerd Fit needs independently reviewed eligibility and safe target policy. |

### Current V3 context

[OFFICIAL MACROFACTOR] A later official update documents optional step-informed expenditure updates and predictive goal adjustment. Step data modifies estimates progressively; it is not a same-day exercise-calorie credit. Predictive goal adjustment accounts for phase changes. These extend V3 rather than replacing it with an independently documented V4. [Expenditure modifiers, updated 2025-11-17](https://macrofactor.com/expenditure-modifiers/)

[UNVERIFIED / PROPRIETARY] Consequently, the blanket claim that current MacroFactor never uses activity or goal information is too broad. Its base expenditure explanation and optional modifiers must be distinguished. Nerd Fit must not imply it implements these proprietary modifiers.

## Original product decisions from this pass

[NERD FIT DESIGN DECISION]

1. Preserve behavioral lessons: fast capture, explicit review of uncertain food, historical recipe snapshots, understandable source priority, and neutral weekly recommendations.
2. Separate observed data, inferred values, recommendations, and accepted targets in both storage and UI. Never make the displayed diary depend on discarding useful partial records.
3. Keep the core deterministic and local; optional AI cannot determine expenditure, silently change goals, or become necessary for routine logging.
4. Define and evaluate a modest original trend/expenditure model before considering sophisticated smoothers. Report calibration/data quality honestly; do not call an uncalibrated score a confidence interval.
5. Do not inherit competitor calorie floors, missing-data thresholds, macro coefficients, physiological claims, search boosts, or algorithm version names as validated Nerd Fit policy.
6. Build original interaction flows and visual identity. Do not copy competitor code, visual arrangement, strings, icons, database packs, or branding regardless of repository license.

## Research limits and next validation

[UNVERIFIED / PROPRIETARY] This pass is a public-document benchmark, not a device usability test, network audit, algorithm reproduction, or independent accuracy comparison. MacroFactor production internals and MacroPhase Android source were not established. Repository tree inspection did not inspect all historical commits or release archives. MacroFactor pages can retain old instructions alongside newer features; the current V3 minimums and later modifier article are explicitly separated above.

[NERD FIT DESIGN DECISION] Before algorithm finalization, use `ALGORITHM_EVIDENCE.md` and `ALGORITHM_SPEC.md` for independent scientific evidence, parameter justification, data-quality rules, simulations, and uncertainty limits. Before food ingestion, use `FOOD_DATA_LICENSES.md` to verify upstream permissions directly. Before claiming comparable UX, run Android capture/edit/restore/check-in flows and measure latency on real devices.
